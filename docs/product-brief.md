# Product brief

## Users

- Product managers deciding when an AI feature may answer customers without a human check.
- Operations leads who staff the human review queue.
- Hiring managers reading this portfolio, who want to see how I reason about AI risk.

## Goal

Make the trade-off between automation, errors and human workload visible and adjustable, using real labelled data instead of example numbers.

## In scope for 0.1.0

- Two models on the same public dataset: a keyword baseline and an AI classifier.
- One global confidence threshold, set by slider, by error target or by cost.
- Per-intent view and real example questions at the chosen threshold.

## Not in scope

- Uploading your own data or model.
- Large language models.
- Per-intent thresholds (planned).
