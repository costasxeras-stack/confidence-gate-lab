# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (20 November 2026)

### Added
- Threshold explorer for two classifiers (keyword baseline and AI classifier) on the 3,080 Banking77 test questions.
- Trade-off chart for both models. Tap or drag on it to set the threshold.
- Threshold pickers by maximum error rate and by cost of wrong answers against human checks.
- Per-intent table and real example questions for the chosen threshold.
- Case study, product brief and three decision records.
