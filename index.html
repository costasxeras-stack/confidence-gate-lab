When should the AI answer on its own, and when should a person check?

Two classifiers sort 3,080 real bank customer questions into 77 intents. Every prediction comes with a confidence score. Move the threshold to choose which answers go out automatically and which go to a person, and see what each choice costs.

Data: Banking77 test set (PolyAI, CC BY 4.0). Each question has one correct intent, labelled by people. Models trained on the 10,003-question training set.
